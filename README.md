WELCOME TO NATURE WALK, a text-based adventure/story game.
You are stressed out, and long for nature. City life is just. Not. It.
Play this game and see what happens at Shollow Park's Trails!

There are two trails-- one is meant to be easier than the other.
Complete the story, discover different endings, and earn up to 3 badges! Bonus points if you discover and ending that is still being work on *winks*

Original Goal: find out what happened to all of the creatures in the park. 

New Goal (since the story is still being worked on): earn all 3 badges!

How to play: 
-select game mode (use either all caps or all lowercase.)
-start walking down the path, and follow the text-prompts to "make decisions."
-meet creatures. Make sure that you brought an empty tea cup with a trusty lid! Some creatures will steal your tea. Keep in mind that you have to work to gather your tea. Be sure to meet that adorable baby fox!
-you are travelling to the end of the pathway, as far as you can make it.
-there might be other travellers, too! Decide whether or not you can trust them.

Part of the WIP part of the game:
-if your teacup shatters, you lose and cannot store your tea. The tea will seep back into the earth, where the secrets that you've missed out on will forever live on. (Spoiler: you can retry by replaying the game, but you cannot save your game, nor can you regain your spilt tea.)
-why should you even try to win? Well, don't we all love a little lore? The mysteries of the forest have long been suspected to hold the key to inner peace. In a world of chaos, who can refuse such the appealling offer?? Not you, at least in this game.
