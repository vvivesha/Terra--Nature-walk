# Terra--Nature-walk
Goal: find out what happened to all of the creatures in the park. Beware! Not all creatures are friendly, and the worst are overtalkative...  :0
How to play: 
-select game mode
-start walking down the path
-meet creatures. Make sure that you brought an empty tea cup with a trusty lid! Some creatures will steal your tea. Keep in mind that you have to work to gather your tea.
-you are travelling to the end of the pathway, as far as you can make it.
-there might be other travellers, too! Decide whether or not you can trust them.
-if your teacup shatters, you lose and cannot store your tea. The tea will seep back into the earth, where the secrets that you've missed out on will forever live on. (Spoiler: you can retry by replaying the game, but you cannot save your game, nor can you regain your spilt tea.)
-why should you even try to win? Well, don't we all love a little lore? The mysteries of the forest have long been suspected to hold the key to inner peace. In a world of chaos, who can refuse such the appealling offer??

The wind is always by your side!!
